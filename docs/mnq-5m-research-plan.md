### Exact SMA reference

Preserve `configs/baseline.yaml` and its saved-run behavior. The preset uses fast/slow SMA 20/50, Wilder ATR(14), a 1× ATR protective stop and both directions. A completed close that moves fast from at/below slow to above signals long, with the reverse for short. Entry and opposite-crossover exits execute at the next observed open, with no immediate reversal. The stop distance uses signal ATR rounded up to a tick and is anchored to the slipped fill. Optional trailing defaults off and, when enabled with its defaults, activates at +2R and trails the best close by 1R. The preset uses $10,000 starting equity, 2% risk sizing, up to two contracts, a $3,000 margin reserve per contract, $1 per side, one adverse tick per fill, and a 10% permanent run drawdown halt. Existing positions can carry overnight and remain marked at an ordinary run's end. [Strategy specification](strategy.md)

## 3. Strategy specifications and initial search

Use a common completed-bar decision interface with typed per-family parameters. At 09:30 ET reset per-session flags, entry counts, cooldowns, opening-range state and VWAP. Orders queue for the next scheduled open after the decision and expire if that slot is missing or outside the entry window. Use one open position and one pending entry at most. Count filled entries. A stop or other exit requires a fresh setup for re-entry. A two-bar cooldown, where specified, means two full completed bars after exit before a new signal. Cancel entry intents at cutoff. Schedule liquidation at the 15:55 open by queuing it at the 15:55 completed-bar timestamp, before the next bar opens at that clock time. Keep the scheduled order ahead of fresh signals. For a future shortened-session policy use close minus five minutes, with its own tested schedule.

Use signal-time Wilder ATR(14) over consecutive five-minute native contract bars, with positive finite ATR and the normal prior-close true range. Protective stops are ATR distance from the actual entry fill with outward tick rounding. Intraday VWAP is the cumulative sum of typical price `(H+L+C)/3` times traded volume divided by cumulative traded volume since 09:30. This is an OHLCV proxy for trade-level VWAP. Invalid or stale inputs suppress new signals with a reason. An existing position retains its stop and scheduled exit. Missing prior slots are excluded from the history count. A rolling lookback requires the specified number of eligible complete observations available at the decision, with no filling from later dates.

|---|---|---|

Later test cutoff and exit interaction only after the coarse study. |
| **B. Time-of-day volatility-adjusted momentum**. A move unusually large for its time slot may reflect persistent demand. | At each half-hour slot 10:00–15:00, compute mean absolute return from the 09:30 open to that slot across the previous N complete sessions of the same contract. Today's bands are open × (1 ± multiplier × mean absolute return). Go long above upper band or short below lower band, with optional default-on VWAP confirmation in the same direction. Arm each side after its condition is false on a decision slot. Exit on a completed close returning to the band or crossing the adverse side of VWAP when enabled, plus stop and scheduled exit. Estimate the slot bands on the five-minute grid so an exit need not wait for an entry slot. | **C. VWAP mean reversion**. Temporary price pressure may reverse when the path is choppy. | Bands are current cash VWAP ± width × five-minute ATR. Arm long when a close falls below its lower band, then enter on a later close back inside the bands and below VWAP. Mirror above the upper band and above VWAP for shorts. ## 5. Regimes, stops and execution

- **Regime reporting first.** At the signal record prior completed cash ATR(14)/prior close, current 12-bar cash efficiency, sign of the move from the cash open, and New York time bucket (before 11:00, 11:00–14:00, from 14:00). Use inner-training terciles for the continuous dimensions, freeze them for the fold, and put early/invalid features in an explicit unavailable bucket. Report one dimension at a time with trade/day counts and uncertainty. A later regime filter is a new trial, with no-trade days retained in daily returns. Treat the existing mean-reversion efficiency gate as part of the initial specification and its ablation as a distinct experiment.
- **Trailing extension.** Test for A and B first. Capture signal ATR at entry as the fixed trail scale and set R from actual fill to initial stop. At a completed close at or beyond +1R, activate. Track post-fill completed highs for longs and lows for shorts from entry, and propose a stop 2 entry ATR behind the extreme. Round long stops down and short stops up. Ratchet only, using the existing native protective order. If the proposed stop is at or through the current close, leave the working stop in place and queue a next-open market exit with a distinct reason. Log activation, proposals, native acknowledgments and fills. Compare off versus on for at most two entry/initial-stop settings per family, then consider activation {1,2}R and distance {1.5,2,2.5} ATR under a separate budget. Compare P&L, drawdown, expectancy, turnover and giveback from MFE. Defer for C and D because their VWAP or scheduled exits leave less opportunity to study a ratchet with five-minute bars.
- **Precedence.** The stop already active gets the current bar's fill opportunity. A fill closes the position and suppresses further exit actions. At the completed close, a risk halt, roll or scheduled liquidation precedes a signal exit, which precedes a trail amendment. Native order state governs cancel/replace, no duplicate liquidation and no unprotected amendment gap. The new stop is effective from the next bar. If that bar opens through the stop, verify the fill uses the executable adverse opening price plus modeled friction. Record trigger and realized prices separately. Test whether the pinned NautilusTrader behavior delivers that convention before treating stops as verified.
- **Execution realism.** Keep UTC for ordering and New York for cash eligibility across DST. Use dated contracts and the existing outgoing-session freeze. Use one contract under the common margin cap, with risk reported in dollars and units of initial R. Retain native accounting and test pre-close decisions against opening events. A missing liquidation bar produces an invalid historical run and a prospective incident. Never invent an exit price. Keep the first release free of simultaneous intrabar targets. If targets are added, test conservative stop-first ordering and alternate feasible ordering. Use finer trades or quotes to check gap stops, same-bar extremes, spread and fill latency. MNQ is $2 per point and $0.50 per 0.25-point tick. [CME contract specifications](https://www.cmegroup.com/markets/equities/nasdaq/micro-e-mini-nasdaq-100.contractSpecs.html)
- **Cost stress.** Provisional baseline is $1 commission per side plus one adverse tick per fill. Stress at $1.50 plus two ticks and $2 plus four ticks. The current native runner exposes only zero/one tick via `FillModel`. Implement and reconcile higher adverse ticks in the pinned engine before labeling them execution stress. An interim additive post-fill cost scenario must be identified as accounting sensitivity because it cannot change fills or halt timing. Include spread in the adverse-tick allowance unless quotes are modeled separately. Net P&L uses native fills and fees once. Validate fees, spread and time-of-day slippage against observed broker statements and bid/ask data when available.
